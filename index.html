Bio

ROSANNA COSTA COSTA has served as the Governor of the Central Bank of Chile since February 2022 and as a Board member since January 2017, when she was appointed to serve the remainder period of then outgoing governor Rodrigo Vergara. She was confirmed in the position in December 2019 for an additional full 10-year term. Ms. Costa has a bachelor’s degree in business administration with a minor in economics from the Pontifical Catholic University of Chile. Ms. Costa held the position of National Budget Director at the Ministry of Finance from 2010 to 2013. Previously, she was the Economics Program Director at the Liberty and Development Institute (Instituto Libertad y Desarrollo) from 1993 to 2010, where she researched issues related to fiscal and labor policy, modernization of the state, and capital markets. From 1984 to 1992, she worked as an economist at the Central Bank of Chile, in the areas of national accounts, research, and monetary policy. At the time of her appointment to the Board, she had been serving as Deputy Director of the Liberty and Development Institute since June 2014 and was also on the Board of the National Productivity Commission and the Advisory Commission on Technical Education. Ms. Costa was a member of the Presidential Commission on Pension Reform in 2006, the Presidential Commission on Labor and Equity in 2008, and, more recently, the Advisory Council against Conflicts of Interest, Influence Trafficking, and Corruption. Other past positions include Director of the State-owned Firms System (SEP), Board Member of the Senior Public Management System (CADP), Director of Comunidad Mujer, Member of the Icare Human Resources Circle, and Member of the Governing Council of the Master’s Program in Management and Public Policy at the Adolfo Ibáñez University. She has taught classes in economics at the Pontifical Catholic University of Chile and recently in diploma programs at the University of Chile.

ALBERTO NAUDON DELL’ORO has been Deputy Governor of the Central Bank of Chile since January 8, 2026, and will serve until March 22, 2028.
